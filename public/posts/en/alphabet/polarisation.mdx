---
title: "Polarisation"
subtitle: "The orientation of a transverse wave oscillation"
author: "Melin Kayapınar"
date: "2026-08-14"
letter: "P"
---

Polarisation describes the orientation of the oscillation of a wave perpendicular to the direction in which it propagates.

If you take a stretched rope at one end and move it up and down, for example, a wave propagates along the rope. In this case, the oscillation is oriented vertically. This concept applies to all kinds of transverse waves, including light.
