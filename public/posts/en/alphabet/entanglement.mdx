---
title: "Entanglement"
subtitle: "Correlations between quantum systems that cannot be described separately"
author: "Melin Kayapınar"
date: "2026-08-14"
letter: "E"
---

Entanglement is a phenomenon in which a system, when considered as a whole, exists in a well-defined state, while the states of its subsystems cannot be precisely determined.

This has astounding consequences. If measurements are conducted on all subsystems, each individual result is random, but taken together the results are correlated. It is as if two dice always mysteriously landed on the same number when rolled.
