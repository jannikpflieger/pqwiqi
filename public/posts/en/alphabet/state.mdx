---
title: "State"
subtitle: "All information about a physical system at a given time"
author: "Melin Kayapınar"
date: "2026-08-14"
letter: "S"
---

The state of a physical system contains all information about the system at a specific time.

In the case of a single electron, this includes its probability distribution in space and the direction of its spin. The term state is not limited to quantum mechanics; it is also used in classical physics, where it provides information such as position and velocity.

In a system consisting of many particles, the total state can be very complex.
