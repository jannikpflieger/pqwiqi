The Planck constant is a fundamental natural constant. It connects energy and frequency, as well as length and momentum, on the smallest scales.

For quantum mechanical calculations, the Planck constant is very important. It is denoted by the letter $h$ and has a value of approximately $h = 6.626 	imes 10^{-34}$ Js.
