title: "Electron"
subtitle: "A negatively charged particle in the shell of an atom"
author: "Melin Kayapınar"
date: "2026-08-14"
letter: "E"
---

Electrons form the shell of an atom and are responsible for the majority of interactions between an atom and its surroundings.

The electrons in the shell can induce chemical bonds and can also repel or attract other particles. In metals, electrons are delocalised and can move freely through the metal, enabling the flow of electric current.
