title: "Probability Density"
subtitle: "A way to describe where a quantum particle is likely to be found"

---

Quantum objects generally do not have a fixed location. Instead, these particles exist within areas with high probability density.

One example is the electron orbitals of atoms, where the electrons are delocalised. The probability density describes the probability of finding a particle at a specific location.
