Absolute zero is the lowest theoretical temperature in our universe. At absolute zero, all matter solidifies, and not even electrons, protons, or neutrons display any movement.

In reality, however, it is impossible to achieve this condition perfectly. Nevertheless, physicists are constantly attempting to reach temperatures as close to absolute zero as possible.
