---
title: "Superconductivity"
subtitle: "A low-temperature state with zero electrical resistance"
author: "Melin Kayapınar"
date: "2026-08-14"
letter: "S"
---

At temperatures below the transition temperature of a superconducting material, the electrical resistance of the material drops to zero, and it exhibits perfect diamagnetism. This means magnetic fields cannot exist within the interior of the material.

Superconductivity can only be explained through the quantum mechanical behaviour of electrons at low temperatures. In these conditions, the electrons exist in a kind of communal macroscopic quantum state that enables this behaviour.

The development of new superconductors is of great importance in solid-state physics, because superconductors have many useful applications.
