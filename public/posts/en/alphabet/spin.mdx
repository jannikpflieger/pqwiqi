Spin is the term used to describe the intrinsic angular momentum of a particle.

In contrast to classical angular momentum, the spin of a particle does not stem from the movement of matter along a circular orbit. Instead, spin is an intrinsic property of every single particle.

Spin is expressed as a multiple of the Planck constant. Particles possess either integer spin, making them bosons, or half-integer spin, making them fermions.
