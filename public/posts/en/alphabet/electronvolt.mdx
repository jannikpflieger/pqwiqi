An electronvolt (eV) is a unit of measure often used in atomic and nuclear physics.

It is defined as the amount of energy that an electron gains when it is subjected to an electric potential difference of 1 volt. This amount of energy, about $1.6 	imes 10^{-19}$ joules, is so tiny that the unit eV is generally irrelevant in daily life.
