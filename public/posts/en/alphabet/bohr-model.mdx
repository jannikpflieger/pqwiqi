The Bohr model is an outdated illustration of an atom from the early days of quantum mechanics. It was used to explain why electrons in an atom can only absorb and emit specific amounts of energy.

The model postulates that electrons orbit the atomic nucleus on stable circular paths, each with a specific radius. Although this model is useful for visualising energy levels in an atom, it is now considered obsolete.
