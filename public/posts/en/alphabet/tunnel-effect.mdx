---
title: "Tunnel Effect"
subtitle: "The quantum chance to pass through a barrier"
author: "Melin Kayapınar"
date: "2026-08-14"
letter: "T"
---

In classical physics, a particle can only overcome a barrier if it has sufficient energy to surmount it.

In contrast, due to the phenomenon of superposition, a quantum particle always has a small probability of already being on the other side of the barrier without requiring the energy to overcome it. This phenomenon is known as the tunnel effect, because the particle appears to have tunnelled underneath the barrier instead of going over the top.
