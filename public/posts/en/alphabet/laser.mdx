title: "Laser"
subtitle: "A source of coherent, monochromatic light"

---

A laser generates light that is monochromatic, meaning single-coloured, and coherent, meaning the light waves have an identical phase. This happens through stimulated emission.

In this process, a photon hits an atom in an excited state. The atom then emits a second, identical photon before returning to its ground state. With an input of energy, the atoms can be stimulated over and over again, enabling the output of many identical photons that amplify the process.
