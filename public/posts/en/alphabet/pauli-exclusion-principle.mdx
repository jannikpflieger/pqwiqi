title: "Pauli Exclusion Principle"
subtitle: "Why identical fermions cannot share the same quantum state"
author: "Melin Kayapınar"
date: "2026-08-14"
letter: "P"
---

The Pauli exclusion principle describes the phenomenon that two or more fermions, particles with half-integer spin, cannot have identical quantum mechanical states.

In practice, this means that two electrons can only occupy the same orbital if they differ in at least one quantum number, such as spin, angular momentum, or energy.
