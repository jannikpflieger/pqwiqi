subtitle: "Why fragile quantum behaviour disappears in everyday systems"

When considering the strange properties of quantum objects, the question arises: why do we not observe these effects in our everyday lives? Why is it not possible for a cat to be in two places at the same time, while electrons can be?

The answer is decoherence. In large systems such as a cat or a human in a non-shielded environment, innumerable quantum particles constantly influence each other. Each of these interactions can be interpreted as a measurement, which in turn destroys the involved states of superposition.
