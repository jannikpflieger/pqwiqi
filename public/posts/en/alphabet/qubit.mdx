The term qubit is short for quantum bit. A qubit is created by a quantum mechanical system with two possible states.

A classical binary bit only has two states, 0 and 1. Quantum mechanics enables a qubit to be in a superposition of both states. A measurement of a qubit, however, always gives 0 or 1 as the result.
