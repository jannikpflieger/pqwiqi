A light quantum, the smallest possible amount of light, is called a photon.

A photon has a specific energy and therefore a specific wavelength, because the wavelength depends on the energy. A photon is a quantum mechanical particle with properties of both a wave and a particle.

Photons can be absorbed and emitted by charged particles such as electrons and protons. In this way, they mediate electromagnetic interactions.
