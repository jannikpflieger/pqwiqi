author: "Melin Kayapınar"

---

Since the first postulation of its principles in the 16th century, classical physics has enabled us to understand and describe our everyday world. Its predictions are intuitive, and its results are very precise within human scales.

In reality, however, our world obeys the laws of quantum mechanics and relativity. The predictions made on very small scales, very large scales, and at high speeds are difficult for humans to comprehend. Nevertheless, these laws enable us to describe our universe much more precisely than classical physics alone can.
