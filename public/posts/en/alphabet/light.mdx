Generally speaking, light is a form of electromagnetic radiation. Visible light is radiation with wavelengths between about 400 and 800 nanometres. The colour of light is determined by its wavelength.

Other types of electromagnetic radiation, such as microwaves, radio waves, ultraviolet light, infrared light, and many more, are basically colours of light that we cannot see.

The smallest particle of light is called a photon. Every photon has a specific energy determined by its wavelength.
