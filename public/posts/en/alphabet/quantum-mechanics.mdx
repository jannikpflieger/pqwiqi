Quantum mechanics is a branch of physics that describes objects and processes at and below the scale of atoms.

It is named after the discovery that initiated the field: the quantisation of fundamental physical properties such as light and energy. Quantum mechanics enables the precise characterisation of many phenomena that were previously impossible to fully comprehend, including heat radiation, semiconductors, the existence of atoms, and the photoelectric effect.
