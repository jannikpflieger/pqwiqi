Particles are usually very small objects. Examples include electrons, atoms, photons, nucleons, and many more. Some particles themselves consist of further particles.

In classical physics, a particle is usually regarded as a point object with a precisely defined position, mass, and speed. In quantum mechanics, particles are more difficult to describe, because their exact position and speed are generally not known. Instead, their individual states are described by wave functions.
