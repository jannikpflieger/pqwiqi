---

A wave is an oscillating movement that propagates into the surrounding area. One example is sound: oscillating air molecules bump into their neighbours, spreading the oscillation through the air.

Another example is light, which is an electromagnetic wave. In this case, the oscillation is the regular periodic alteration of electric and magnetic fields.
