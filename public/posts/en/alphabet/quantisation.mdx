title: "Quantisation"
subtitle: "The idea that some physical quantities come in discrete units"
author: "Melin Kayapınar"

letter: "Q"
---

Quantisation is the term and phenomenon on which quantum mechanics is based.

Contrary to classical physics, it was discovered that many physical properties previously thought to be arbitrarily divisible are actually quantised. One example is light, which can be split into individual photons, each with a specific minimum amount of energy.

One way of envisioning this is pixels on a screen. From a distance, you might think it is possible to zoom into an image indefinitely, but when you get close enough you realise that the image is composed of individual pixels: the smallest units of the image.
