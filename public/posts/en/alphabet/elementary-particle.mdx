Elementary particles are subatomic particles which, to our current knowledge, are not composed of other particles. They are the fundamental building blocks of all matter in our universe.

The Standard Model of particle physics contains quarks, leptons, and gauge bosons, which are responsible for fundamental interactions. In addition, there is the Higgs boson, which is partly responsible for the mass of other particles.

Quarks and leptons, especially up quarks, down quarks, and electrons, make up the matter in our everyday surroundings.
