---
title: "Interference"
subtitle: "What happens when waves overlap"
author: "Melin Kayapınar"
date: "2026-08-14"
letter: "I"
---

When two waves encounter each other, they can amplify or diminish each other. This effect is called interference.

For example, if you throw two stones into a lake, you can observe interference between the two ring-shaped waves. Wave crests can become higher and troughs deeper. If a crest meets a trough, however, they can cancel each other.

Interference can be observed in all kinds of waves, such as sound, light, and even quantum particle waves.
