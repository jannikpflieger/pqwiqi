---
title: "Macroverse & Microverse"
subtitle: "The difference between everyday scales and microscopic detail"
author: "Melin Kayapınar"
date: "2026-08-14"
letter: "M"
---

The macroverse is the world in which we live. We do not need to determine the specific location of each individual atom in a lump of iron. Instead, it is sufficient to know a few macroscopic properties such as size, weight, and temperature.

The microverse, however, is very precise. To describe a microscopic system, the state of every single particle has to be known. If, for example, we want to describe a hydrogen atom, we need to know the precise states of both the electron and the proton.
