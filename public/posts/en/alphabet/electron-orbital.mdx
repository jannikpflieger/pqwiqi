Electron orbitals are the current model for visualising the locations of electrons in an atom. Each orbital is occupied by a maximum of two electrons with opposite spins.

The various orbitals have different energy levels. The orbital with the lowest energy is occupied first, with other orbitals filled according to increasing energy until all electrons are accounted for.

Within an orbital, the electrons are delocalised. This is often visualised as an electron cloud filling the whole orbital. The orbitals represent probability distributions for the locations of the electrons.
