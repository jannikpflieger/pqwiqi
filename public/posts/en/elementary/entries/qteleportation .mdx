# Quantum Teleportation

The first thing that comes to mind when teleportation is mentioned is the fact that teleporting ourselves from one place to another. Although there is still a long way to go to achieve this dream of ours, quantum teleportation is possible.

## Magic Coins
Imagine Alice and Bob have a pair of magic twin coins. These coins are so closely linked that whatever changes occur to one is instantly reflected in the other, no matter how far apart they are.
It is easier to visualize if Alice’s coin lands tails in Munich, Bob’s coin lands heads in Berlin, too, even though no one touched it. Because of this, if we disturb Alice, Bob will be affected regardless of the distance.

## Secret Message
Now, a friend named Charlie gives Alice a secret message. This could be a secret color that Alice wants to send Bob. Unfortunately, since she cannot look at the color or it will fade away, she cannot learn the color by looking at it and mail it to Bob. Instead, she performs a comparison test between her magic particle and the secret.

## Comparison Test
For better understanding, imagine Alice putting a magic die and Charlie’s secret color into a machine that tells her how different they are. This process causes magic to disappear, but gives Alice the clues (a code).

## Bob learns the key
This test destroys the original magic between Bob’s and Alice’s coin, but it creates a key. Alice calls Bob to tell him about this key. When Bob learns about the key, he uses it to "tweak" his own coin until it reveals the secret message Alice wanted him to have.
