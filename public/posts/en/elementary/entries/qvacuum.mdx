# Quantum Vacuum 
### "Is Empty Space Actually Empty?"

![Quantum Vacuum](posts/assets/entries/qvacuum/qvacuum.png)

Imagine have a magical box and a magical vacuum cleaner that removes everything inside. There is no dust, no air, no atoms, no electrons, no protons, no neutrons, and not a single particle of light. Absolutely nothing remains. 

Now you ask yourself: Is this box completely empty? Does it have any particles inside? 

Well you might think it is meaningless to ask such a question, since everything was removed form the box using magic vacuum. But in the quantum world, the answer is surprisingly **no**. 

Even in a perfect vacuum, something strange keeps happening inside the box all the time. If you had a magical remote which can hit a pause button inside the box and see what's going on in there at different moments you would have found that tiny particle (inconceivable to the human eye of course) suddenly appears and then suddenly disappears. This keeps happening again and again and in a flash of a moment. It's like a never ending game of superfast peek-a-boo. Scientists call this game of peek-a-boo as **Quantum Fluctuation**. 

But now you might ask how is this happening? What is the underlying principle which allows this kind of strange process? 

In the quantum world there is a very fascinating phenomena which allows creation of a tiny amount of energy out of nowhere that disappears instantly. The famous Einstein equation $$E=mc^2$$ tells us that energy and matter are connected. In other sense, we have created some particle with some mass out of nowhere! Now putting it all together, a tiny bit of energy pops up which creates a pair of particles (they appear in pairs because of some deeper rules of the quantum world, something we’ll explore in the more advanced section) and then they vanish almost instantly. 
	
Coming back to our original question, what does “empty” actually mean? We usually think empty means there is nothing inside. However, quantum physics tells us that even empty space is full of activity. Empty space has energy, motion and tiny events constantly taking place.
But either way this idea is incredible because it means the universe is actually never completely still and “nothing” is not quite what it seems. So the next time you think of an empty space, remember it is alive with subtle quantum activity.
