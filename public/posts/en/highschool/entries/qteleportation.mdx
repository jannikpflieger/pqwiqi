# Quantum Teleportation

The first thing that comes to mind when teleportation is mentioned is the fact that teleporting ourselves from one place to another. Although there is still a long way to go to achieve this dream of ours, quantum teleportation is possible. It is an interesting reality of quantum physics that is used in quantum computers for several purposes. 

## Quantum Information
For better understanding, imagine two particles that are separate from each other. Let’s refer to these as Alice’s particle is particle A and Bob’s particle is particle B. The fundamental purpose of quantum teleportation is to teleport an unknown quantum state from one place to another that is separated by long distances. A quantum state provides a detailed description of a particle, such as an electron or a proton.

## Quantum Entanglement
The teleportation of a quantum state is possible if and only if Alice and Bob are entangled. This is all about particle A having a correlation with particle B. As a result, if Alice measures her qubits, Bob will be affected regardless of the distance. Since the measurement changes particle A, particle B will inevitably be transformed. It is important to note that since entanglement is a property of quantum mechanics, teleportation is only possible in quantum computers.
Another essential part of this is also a third particle named Charlie’s particle, particle C. This is the particle that carries an unknown quantum state, which will later be teleported to Bob. Performing Bell measurement is the next step. This is a requirement because there is not enough classical information to reconstruct this particle.

## Bell Measurement
This is a joint measurement of a pair of particles; in this case, particles A and C. While this breaks the entanglement between particle A and particle B, particle A and particle C are now entangled. The result of this measurement doesn’t really reveal any information about the unknown quantum state; rather, it just gives clues about particle C. This measurement outcome is classical information that is obtained from quantum information

## Encoding Information
Because of this measurement, particle B has changed. Now Alice can share the classical information via classical channels, which indicates that information cannot travel faster than light. This is a crucial point because, although right now particle B carries an unknown quantum state, its current state is just a mixture of other quantum information.
Hence, without Alice sharing the outcomes classically, Bob is unable to decode his qubit (to eliminate unwanted information) to obtain the desired state. Much like in classical computers that work with binary codes 1 and 0, Alice's measurement results in one of four specific classical combinations: (00, 01, 10, or 11).
Depending on the information Bob obtained, he can apply operations to his particle, thereby transforming particle B into particle C, which carries an unknown quantum state. Now, all the information particle C carried had been teleported to particle B. It is important to transfer this quantum information without losing any quantum information content.

## Simplification
Now, assume that Charlie gives Alice a mysterious black box. She doesn’t know what is inside, and since she cannot open it. However, she doesn’t care because doing so would make the content inside disappear forever.
With the help of entanglement, Alice performs a 'Bell Measurement,' and the box's contents are instantly 'teleported' to Bob. But when Bob looks at the teleported box, unfortunately, he doesn't have the original Black Box. Instead, he has a new box that is making noise when it is shaken.
## Where is Charlie’s box?
Bob knows that he has the black box, but it’s mixed with other unwanted boxes in the new box. To get the black box, he must know the correct configuration to open. We could think of a Rubik's Cube that has been twisted.
## Correct Key
Alice sends a classical text message (the 'key') telling Bob: Rotate the bottom twice and the right side once.
## Result
Bob follows Alice’s instructions. He now holds the original, unopened Black Box. It is important to note that Bob still doesn't know what is inside. He just knows that he now possesses the exact same mysterious black box Charlie started with. He can now pass this box into a quantum computer to do a calculation, all without ever 'opening' it and ruining the secret.
